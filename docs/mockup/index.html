Research

Verify, don't trust.

AI today is confidently — and unaccountably — wrong. As it moves into safety-critical work, “we're fairly sure it's fine” stops being good enough. My work asks a sharper question: can we know, with a formal guarantee, that an AI system did exactly what we asked? I approach it through faithful formalization and formal verification of machine reasoning — so trust rests on a checker we can prove correct, not on the model.
